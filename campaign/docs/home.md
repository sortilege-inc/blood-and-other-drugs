Toronto has rules about the thin-blooded. Other cities are kinder. Nobody who holds a seat at the Prince's
court may be one, or have made one, and when one of them meets the Final Death, the court does not call it
murder.

Four of them are still here. A street doctor with a bad French translation of a worse Persian book. A bail
bondsman who puts himself between his sire and whatever is happening. A handsome man who fed like a sandman
until he found a bone in an alley. A kid who finds empty buildings, and whose body is somebody else's still.

They have a park now. Church and Gloucester, given with full writ, for a bone that was worth five million
dollars and then was stolen out from under a Tremere. The Prince knows it's theirs. The Sheriff knows their
faces. Something that can walk through wards is loose in the city, and the old blood wants it found.
