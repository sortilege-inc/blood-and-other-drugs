---
name: The University ballroom
region: The Prince's ground
---

A rentable room at the University, the kind weddings and corporate events use, fancy in a different way from the chantry. For one night it was
Elysium: about a hundred people, most of them ghouls, fewer than half of them Kindred. The Sheriff at the door for the first couple of hours; then the
doors locked, and you could leave but nobody else got in. A lounge of vessels, for tasting. The Prince holding court at a table. Four hours, start to
finish, and nobody arrived on time.

The Sheriff keeps his base up near the University of Toronto. It's all pretty close together, up there.
