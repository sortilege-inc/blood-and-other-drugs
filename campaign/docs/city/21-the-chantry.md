name: The Chantry
region: The Prince's ground
---

In one of the old stone buildings by the library, near St. Joseph's, up toward Wellesley and Queen's Park. The door is iron and looks retired;
its handle is a magnet, and if you know where to set it, something inside clicks. No phones past the car.

Inside there is a garden growing in the middle of the building, and a gallery over it, and a short stair to what ought to be storage and is
instead carpet, paintings, soft light, and a ghoul in good clothes to take your coat. Tomes open on stands. Brass instruments set at angles, tracking
planets or something else. Low shelves of vampire novels, sorted from *nothing here is true* on the left to *someone's head should roll* on the right.
A human foot, part bone and part replica, labelled *The Foot of the Methuselah*.

It is Dominik's, and [[14-dominik|Dominik]] is Tremere. The Sheriff carried Malik through its door on fire. Something came through it after that,
through every ward and guard, and took the bone. The door was repaired within days. You'd never know.
