Distilleries, light manufacturing, and alleys down toward the lake, as near as the expressway lets you get. There's a lot where trucks dump their heaps
to be picked up, and a plant that opens before dawn.

It is where the Sunburners dug a grave for nine, and waited for sunup. Eight of them went to ash. See [[07-the-litter]].
