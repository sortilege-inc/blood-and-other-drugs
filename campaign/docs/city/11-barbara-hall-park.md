Church Street and Gloucester, in Church–Wellesley. Dominik gave it to Rose for the bone, with full writ: feeding grounds, his protection,
passage through the Camarilla's city, and leave to share it with those under her protection. There hadn't been much activity there lately.
The Camarilla, he said, could use someone holding down the fort.

It's low buildings, two to four storeys. Shops at street level, apartments over the shops, a few proper apartment blocks, almost no houses.
The low windows have grilles that open from inside and not from out, so getting in is the question; once in, there are floors and floors of
people asleep.

[[02-ethan-cole|Ethan]] is moving his business into a building here, over a basement with no windows. [[03-ralph-begg|Ralph]] has an apartment a few blocks
away, in a building with eleven other units. The Sunburners asked if they could play here sometimes. *As long as you play nice.*

There is something under it: a line of something running through the ground, which each of them felt in their own way. Nobody gives up
prime ground. Maybe it was given to them for a reason.

The Prince knows it's theirs. He won't be visiting.
