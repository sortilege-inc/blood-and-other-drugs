[[21-evelyn|Evelyn Skye]]'s barony, with some contested ground around its edges where she and the Camarilla proper rub up against each other. Rose
works here. Blood is played with here, carefully and together, or not at all: the frat-house lab, with its Greek letters half fallen off the front and
its towers grinding in the basement, was shut down on Evelyn's word.
