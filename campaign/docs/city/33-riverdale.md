The barony to the northeast: Father Vivek's. Trieste spoke of a crew in East York who had fought the Sheriff before. More tolerant of Trieste's people than most, she says, only they had a falling out, and making it up would take
something like an apology. His name is on Ethan's short list too.

At Elysium he sat with three people in matching teal scarves. One of them invited Ethan to the temple's New Year's party, at an address that turned out
to be a community centre. Kindred-friendly. Bring your friends.

The coterie has only seen him across a room.
