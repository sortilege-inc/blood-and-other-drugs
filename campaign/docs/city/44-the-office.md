Not a good part of town, which is the point. A regular office from the front; behind the glass door, a second door of bars. Roll-down steel shutters on
the windows. A holding cell with a bunk and a toilet. A heavy door on a room of body armour and weapons that are mostly not lethal. Cameras on everything.
Break in and you'd get there, eventually, but the whole neighbourhood would hear you.

Ralph slept in the cell, with the door unlocked. The bail calls come in after the bars close.

It's moving to [[11-barbara-hall-park|Barbara Hall Park]]. See [[04-the-coag]].
