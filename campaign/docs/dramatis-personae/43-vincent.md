Bald, big ears, too many teeth: Count Orlok, out of the old silent film. He made Ralph, and when Ralph didn't come out ugly he broke
his neck and left him. Ralph had him for about an hour.

They met again at Elysium, where Vincent had come on pressing business with the Prince; someone destroyed his haven, and he has to
rebuild. He knew Ralph at once. *What do you want of me? My bloodline ends with me. You have your looks still.* Then, when Ralph named
Barbara Hall Park: *you have exceeded my expectations. Considerably.*

He lives on the harbourfront now, by Ireland Park, and keeps to himself. He looked at Ralph's drawing and shuddered: *that is a cursed
face. You do not wish to fight that face.* He pointed Ralph at Evelyn Skye.

If Ralph doesn't visit within a few weeks, Vincent says, he'll come and pay him one.

He once had five or six ghouls. One of them was [[64-simon|Simon]].
