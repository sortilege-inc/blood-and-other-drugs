A serious scholar of the occult, Rose says, and very influential; close to the Prince. His chantry is behind an iron door
near the library, with a garden in the middle of the building and carpet where the storage ought to be, and tomes open on
stands, and brass instruments set at particular angles.

Claude knew him first, five years ago, as a man in a good coat who talked about legacy. That is her story; see [[01-the-gate-of-flesh]].

The rest of them met him over the bone. He judged it genuine, *Tzimisce Haudenosaunee*, a potent curse or spell, and gave Rose
Barbara Hall Park for it, with his protection and passage in the Camarilla's city. Then he lost it. Now he expects it back.
Rose passed on a rumour, not to be repeated to him: that he once made a childe who came out thin-blooded, and had it killed.

He looks at the coterie with contempt, mostly. Bring him something interesting and it changes: the husk from the morgue
got them questions, and answers, and *occasionally you seem to have some worth*. Rami got an hour and a half of rapt attention.
He hums to himself when he works. Ethan has heard the tune somewhere before.
