If Elysium were a wedding, Sabine would be the planner. Everything goes smoothly; violence stays outside. Rose told
Ethan to congratulate her on, as always, a wonderful Elysium, and he did, and she said she loved courteous guests.

She also said that Rose's judgement kept her in Evelyn's camp, and that it stung a little. And that she hoped Ethan
wouldn't suffer the same fate as Rose's *previous* childe. Rose had never mentioned a previous childe. *Probably better
left forgotten*, said Sabine.

She was kinder to Ralph. She'd expected him to look different, as Vincent's childe, and said so. She introduced him to
the Prince herself. When he said his goal was to be remembered but not thought of too much by those in power, she offered
to teach him how to help run the next Elysium.
