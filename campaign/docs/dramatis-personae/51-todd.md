Olly knew him from before. *It's been a crazy year for me*, Todd said, and showed a fang.

He was working the centrifuge in the frat-house lab in Kensington, the operation that had been told twice to move and wouldn't. He
tried to calm things down when Rose came in, and sat when Ethan told him to sit. He got out with his head, he says, and not much else.
The towers and the drives went up the stairs in Ethan's arms.

At the COAG he had a suitcase of little bottles, each one a mood, and he was trying to go independent. He let Olly pick one: the
melancholic. *I call that one the Strength of Sadness. It's good for when you need your defences up.* He wanted lab time, if Olly
could get it.

Rose says he did not heed repeated advisories after multiple infractions.
