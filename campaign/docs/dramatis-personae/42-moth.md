Claude's teacher, more or less. Looks about thirty, is probably ten years older, and never stays anywhere past the seventh night: the
curse of Moth's line, explained the way someone else might explain a bad knee. So Moth keeps a loop of storage units and warehouses and
little troves, books and bones and things in jars. A magpie with a library card.

Moth reads; Moth doesn't *do*. That's what Claude is for. Every safehouse comes with a new book, and every book with a quiz.

Dominik Levesque once paid Moth a book to destroy Claude. Moth didn't, and told her so, eventually. Moth was shot in the day, in a
safehouse that should have been safe, and Claude came out of hiding and killed for it.
