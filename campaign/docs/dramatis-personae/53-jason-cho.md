A good suit on Ralph's front step, a tablet, and an opening offer of five million dollars for the bone. Warm hand, breathing, blinking,
and no pulse: exactly as dead as Ralph, and wearing it as well. He left a card on the kitchen table. *Jason Cho — Collector.* No number,
no address.

At the COAG he came through the crowd in a straight line, pomaded and pleasant, and asked Ralph whether he had any questions. Ralph told
him he was still the frontrunner.

He wasn't. Rose says he isn't buying for himself; someone sent him. Until they know who, she can't say how much trouble he'll be.
