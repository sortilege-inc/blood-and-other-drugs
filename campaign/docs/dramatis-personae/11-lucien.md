The Prince of Toronto, as far as the Camarilla is concerned, which is to say as far as the Camarilla can make it
stick. At Elysium he held court at a table, literally and otherwise: people came, sat or stood, and when he gave a
little flick of the wrist they were done, and went.

[[12-sabine|Sabine]] walked Ralph up to him on her arm and introduced him as [[43-vincent|Vincent]]'s childe. The Prince
had just been talking with Vincent, at length. *You have impeccable connections, it seems. Mind your manners and you
will have a good stay.*

Rose brought Ethan up later, in the queue like everyone else. He knew about Barbara Hall Park. *Keep that in mind.
Thank you for making me aware. You may go.* Ethan took it to mean the Prince would not be going to that park, and
that this was, from him, the height of courtesy.

He didn't kill either of them. [[12-sabine|Sabine]] called that a good start.
