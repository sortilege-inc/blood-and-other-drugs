Trieste's crew. He swigs something from a flask, then licks a lighter, and the fire fills his mouth and runs down his throat like it
lives there, and he breathes it out on whoever is closest.

The Sheriff took him from the COAG with [[32-lil-sis|Lil Sis]]. When Olly's car broke them loose, Malik got up and wrapped the Sheriff
in a bear hug and set them both alight. Olly beat at the flames with his coat, and Malik fought to keep them going. The Sheriff took
him into the chantry still burning.

*Lab rats*, Rami said, when Olly told him where they were going. Trieste didn't try again. Ralph's view was that a man whose go-to under
stress is setting himself and someone else on fire is not someone you rescue.

Nobody has seen him since.
