The Baron of Kensington, and Rose's sire. Good to thin-bloods, better than most. She likes the ones who play with blood
to do it carefully, and together, and in a way that doesn't bring the Prince down on Kensington, or his Sheriff, or CSIS; the
frat-house lab was closed on her word.

Ethan keeps a careful distance from what she's into: ghosts, spells, the kind of thing he'd rather not have explained to him.
She is strange, he thinks.

At Elysium she spent a long time going over a book with someone whose skin was nearly translucent. [[43-vincent|Vincent]] told Ralph
that she might have resources against the face in his drawing. Rose wants to bring [[41-rami|Rami]] to meet her.
