Egyptian, very calm, and never in a hurry. He made Olly in his own basement lab and offered him a choice between useful and dead, and
he has fed on him carefully since. Sustainably. He puts things into Olly, *precursors*, and takes them out again a week or two later,
changed. He makes one thing, the Kindred answer to ecstasy, for customers he plainly despises, and never touches it himself. L is his.

He is Banu Haqim, and he told Olly the curse of his line: when they feed on their own kind, sometimes they cannot stop. It happened once.
Olly shot him three times in the stomach, and he apologized.

There was another before Olly, Hollis, who insulted the Prince. Rami is careful with his tools.

He has avoided the Barons and the Camarilla both, and does his work privately, in his lane, with enormous contempt for anyone who plays
with blood irresponsibly. He told Dominik what he knows about blood alchemy for an hour and a half, and did not grovel. He will meet him,
he said, once.
