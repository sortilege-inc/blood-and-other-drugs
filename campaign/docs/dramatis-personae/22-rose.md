Ethan's sire. Brujah, in Evelyn's service, and old: every so often she drops a word nobody has used in a century. She wanted
Ethan, and she is teaching him even though the blood came out thin. She never texts when an envelope will do, always the same
kind, always the same few words.

She closes down operations that won't move, and does it like weather. She put her thumb under Ralph's lip in York Commons and
called him *a long-lost cousin*. She did the talking at the chantry and came out with a park: territory her people had never had,
with full writ, and she is extending her protection to Ethan and Ralph with it.

She doesn't keep ghouls. She has one now: [[64-simon|Simon]], bound on Ethan's initiative and her blood.

She has business she doesn't explain. [[12-sabine|Sabine]] mentioned a previous childe. Ethan has a bone to pick with her about that.
