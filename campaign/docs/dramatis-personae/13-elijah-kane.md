Six foot plenty, in a suit that is for working in. Ethan was told early who to tip his hat to in this city, and who not
to cross at all; the Sheriff was the second kind. As long as he does what the Prince wants, he can do whatever he wants.

At the COAG he zip-tied [[32-lil-sis|Lil Sis]] and [[33-malik|Malik]] and carried them off, one in each hand, *to Tash*.
Olly hit him with a stolen SUV and then shot him in the head from no distance at all, and he shook it off like a dog shaking
off water. He moves so fast there is no between. He took Malik into the chantry on fire and did not come out.

He remembered Olly at the door of Elysium, and held a candelabrum to his face: *Burned. To a crisp. Just like you will be.*
Olly walked into the flame, and the Sheriff was the one who stepped back.

He will not have forgotten that either.
