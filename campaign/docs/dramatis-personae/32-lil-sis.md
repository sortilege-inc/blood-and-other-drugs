One of Trieste's, and about as old in the blood as Trieste, Olly thinks. She talks about the sun the way other people talk about
rain: *Not since Jonah got crispy. It's going to be all our turns sometime.*

She and [[33-malik|Malik]] cleaned out someone's cache of old books and tried to sell them at the COAG, and the Sheriff took them both.
Olly hit the three of them with a car to get them back, and broke her arm doing it. She stood up anyway and screamed *I want you to
stop*, and for a long moment the Sheriff did.

She rode out in Rose's car and told Rose what she thought of the Camarilla. At the pit she keeps the new ones in line with a shovel,
swung like a baseball bat.
