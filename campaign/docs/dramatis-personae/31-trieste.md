Olly met her at a rave. She can walk into any bar in the city and walk out with the worst man in it, and bring him somewhere
abandoned, and there Olly will be.

Once a month she and her crew go somewhere with a view of the east and sit out and watch the sun come up, and see who doesn't
quite make it back inside. After, they cut what's left with a little L and snort it. *It's a good way to remember someone.* It's
a bonding activity.

She woke up in a pit, she says, with maybe fifteen others, and never saw who put her there. Now she makes her own: nine in a grave
by the Distillery District, a bachelorette party from Saskatchewan, and one of them survived the sun. She's worked out how to cut
Jonah's ashes so she can stay out till eleven in the morning, and she gave the coterie three vials of it, paying back or
paying forward.

She talks like she knows everyone. Some of it is true. Olly can't always tell which parts.
