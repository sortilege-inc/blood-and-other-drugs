Darya does ink. Blood-alchemy ink, the only kind that holds in dead skin. For the thin-bloods who don't want the brand and everything that
comes with it, she does something that passes for one.

At the COAG she was filling every block of the night. Ethan and Ralph each came out of her booth with a few sweeping lines: Rose's mark,
the one that says whose they are. Once Ralph had seen it he saw it on other people all over the room.

Olly went looking for her with photographs of the markings on the husk in the drawer. He got her assistant, and a maybe.
