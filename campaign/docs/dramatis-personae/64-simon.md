Picked up for smashing a beer bottle over a stranger's head and stomping on him; it took three people to pull him off. At Ethan's office he got
belligerent: *What, I'm not good enough for you? All you biters want a little pack following you around like lapdogs.*

In the basement he broke down. *I miss him so much. He abandoned me. He's the ugliest, cruellest person I've ever met. But Vincent abandoned me.*
Vincent had five or six of them, he said: Anne Marie, Linh, Tony, others he couldn't remember. Something in his head stops him saying how he met
Vincent.

Ethan made him an offer, and Rose made it good. He is bound to her now, and working off his charges under Ethan's eye. He might know a great deal
about [[43-vincent|Vincent]].
