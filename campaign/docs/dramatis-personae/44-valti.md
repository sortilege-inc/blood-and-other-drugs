---
name: Valti
circle: The unaligned
epithet: very tall, and alone
first: Tall Women
---

Taller than Claude by a head and more, the far side of seven feet, wrapped in layers against the sun and a hat tipped low. Claude found her
feeding on a dying patient, and she didn't stop for being seen. *I can reduce the corpse to ash. And no one will be any the wiser.* She said
*adieu* and was gone.

She keeps to herself, and people tend to leave her alone. She slept in a condemned apartment building until she couldn't anymore; she didn't
say why. Who made her, she can't say. The change blurred it.

Claude offered her a roof, and a proposition. She said she'd listen.
