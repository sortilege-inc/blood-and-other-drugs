Not Elysium: lower class than that, lower key, run on a gentleman's agreement. A pop-up that moves; if you know, you know. It looks like a small flea market,
or a new-age fair set up in a tunnel: vendors with semi-private booths for real business, and out in the open, crystals and love potions and occult bric-a-brac
for the mortals who come for the edge of it. A lot of talking. A lot of stories.

A hundred Kindred in Toronto, people say, at least, and probably too many. More ghouls than that. Most of the buyers are marks. The interesting ones are the
outliers. See [[04-the-coag]].
