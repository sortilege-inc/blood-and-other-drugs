---
name: The bone
kind: Relics
---

A human femur, carved all the way up and down in something descended from cuneiform. Ralph saw it in a tall man's hand in an alley, and found it behind a garbage
can afterwards. His research turned up *Methuselah's ossuary*: bones like it, four or five in the world, treated as deeply magical. Rose read *horse* on it, and
*lift*, or *threshold*. A ritual object for the dead, or for doing things to the dead.

Jason Cho offered five million for it. Dominik called it *Tzimisce Haudenosaunee*, an incredibly potent spell or curse, and paid a park for it. Then something
went through the chantry's wards and took it back.
