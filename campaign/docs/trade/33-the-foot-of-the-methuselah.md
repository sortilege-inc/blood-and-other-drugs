In Dominik's sitting room: the bones of a human foot, some real and the rest careful replicas filling out the shape, like a dinosaur in a museum. A small plate gives
the title, and a quotation in a language that feels like Latin and isn't. Rami stood in front of it a long time. See [[08-elysium]].
