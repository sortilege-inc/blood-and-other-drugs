---
name: Le Livre Antique du Sang
kind: Relics
---

The Ancient Book of Blood, rendered from Persian into French by Péladan, the occultist, who was not much of a translator. It describes a series of transformations
it calls *gates*. The first is the Gate of Flesh; the second, the Gate of Names.

Dominik paid it to Moth for a job, thinking it worthless. Moth read it, and Claude tried it. See [[01-the-gate-of-flesh]].
