*The furnace of the body.* A living vessel to cook in: one of them, with precursors put into their blood by IV and left for a week or two, and taken
out again changed. The body does the work. The vessel is never high, exactly, but the work has weather: a week wired, a week in a hole with no edges.

Rare. Rami knew of no other in the whole city until the husk turned up in the coroner's drawer, dried out and drained, tattooed, with holes and ports
in the skin where things had gone in. Dominik wants one badly, and pays well. Rose has a general idea, from some subreddits that are a little loose
with their words, of how to make one, if someone brings her a subject.

Rami told Dominik the ways it goes wrong. Olly listened. You can melt from the inside. Your whole throat can burn out.
