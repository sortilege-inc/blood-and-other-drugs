name: Moods in the blood
kind: What is made
---

Blood carries the mood of whoever it came out of. Rami taught Olly that, early, and sends him out with instructions: *find me sanguine blood this week.
Something hot.* Olly got good at reading people, and at feeding against the swing of whatever Rami had cooking in him, a downer for the up weeks, an upper
for the down.

Todd's bottles are sorted by it: one phlegmatic, that gets you drunk; one for sleep with good dreams; one melancholic, the Strength of Sadness. And a
vessel who's very drunk or very high passes it on, which is how Olly got a speedball at Elysium.
