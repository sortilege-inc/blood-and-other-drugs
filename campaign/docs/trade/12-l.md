Rami's. The Kindred answer to ecstasy, as near as Olly can tell, made in small batches for customers Rami plainly despises. He never touches it himself.
It's cooked in an athanor.

Trieste cuts it into the ashes of her dead and snorts them, to remember. See [[01-the-gate-of-flesh]].
