The frat-house lab made something like weed, in blood, in a bottle, carbonated. Don't chug it. By the COAG Todd had a suitcase of little bottles, six or eight
kinds, each needing its own blood and time to brew and a furnace to do it in. The Strength of Sadness, the melancholic one, is *good for when you need your
defences up. Emotionally and otherwise.*

Rami heard the name and started to say it would be helpful, and stopped himself.
