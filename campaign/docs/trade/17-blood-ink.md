The only ink that holds in dead skin is made with blood alchemy. [[52-darya|Darya]] does it.

Thin-blooded and caught on the Camarilla's ground without someone to answer for you, you tend not to leave it. There are two ways around that: walk in behind
someone with enough weight to tell the killers to fuck off, or be registered, branded, for good. Tash does the branding. For those who won't take the brand,
Darya does something that passes for one, and for those who belong to someone, a mark: a few sweeping lines that say whose you are. Ethan and Ralph wear Rose's.
