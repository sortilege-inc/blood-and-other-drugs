name: What an athanor can make
kind: What is made

Rami told Dominik, and so the rest of them heard it: things that can turn you into a bat. Things that let you pass through walls, or sink into the earth.
Something that lets you travel by moonlight to places much farther away, under certain conditions. Something that lets you breathe fire. Malik drinks
something from a flask before he does.

Olly's experience of thin-blood concoctions made with stronger blood is that some of them are no joke, and all of them are temporary; a month is the longest he
knows of. Some spoil in days and have to be fresh. Some are sold as centuries old. Some are fine wine. Some are mayonnaise.

Olly does not know which of these Rami has been brewing in him. See [[08-elysium]].
