There is no scene, officially. Claude found the edges of it through Moth's dealers and their dealers: people doing strange things with blood, all over the city,
no meetups, no names. Evelyn wants it done carefully and together, and has Rose close down what isn't.

Rose's view: anyone can make potions now. They help if you're thin-blooded, and thin-bloods don't have money or boons, so selling to them sucks. The real money is
in artifacts, books, the occult shit: real buyers, real power, real boons. She and Ethan have confiscated a fair amount of paraphernalia over the last seven or eight
months, and she'd like to be Dominik's exclusive supplier.
