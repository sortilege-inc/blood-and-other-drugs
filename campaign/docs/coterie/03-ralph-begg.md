Ralph has been handsome his whole life, and it has got him about as far as handsome gets you. A jock who
couldn't make the next level; a run of dot-com jobs he was good at until someone noticed what else he was
doing with the access; then a con, a job, a con. When California stopped being fun he came home to Toronto
and moved into a friend's basement in the Junction Triangle. [[61-bill|Bill]] is one of the few people he has
never taken advantage of. He means to keep it that way.

Then he skimmed from the wrong app. The thing it belonged to looked like Count Orlok, and it made him, and
when he didn't come out ugly it broke his neck and left him in the gutter. He worked out the rest alone, with
a laptop, the way he has worked out everything.

He feeds like a sandman: quiet streets, clean blood, a jogging suit at three in the morning, in and out, and
nobody wakes. He found the bone in an alley, and he nearly got five million dollars for it, and he traded it
for ground instead. He means to make the trade worth it.

He plans on being here forever, and he does not intend to spend forever at the bottom.
