Claudette, if you want the whole of it. She was thirty, and a doctor who took her clinic to the
people who never came back to the same clinic twice, and she was on the kind of streak you get once
in a career, when a man in a good coat came and asked her what happens to research like hers when the
researcher dies.

He made her. Then he saw what he had made, thin and weak and not what he had wanted, and walked her
across the city and handed her to someone called Moth, with something that was meant to buy her death.
Moth didn't do it. Moth kept her instead, and gave her books, and quizzed her on them.

She has spent five years since then learning to do things with blood out of a bad French translation of
a worse Persian book, and she is getting somewhere. The book describes *gates*. The first is the Gate of
Flesh, and she needed an athanor to open it, and she found [[04-olly|Olly]].

She keeps the rules that are written down. Mostly.
