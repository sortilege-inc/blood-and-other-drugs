Olly finds places. Old bottling plants, tunnels under the rail yards, buildings with every sign of being empty.
He throws parties in them: speakers stacked at one end, drum and bass you can feel in your fillings, a spot like
that you only ever use once. He climbs the sides of buildings he has no business climbing, because he said out
loud that he would, and saying it out loud is how he makes himself do things.

One of the empty buildings wasn't. He woke up strapped to a board in [[41-rami|Rami]]'s lab, dead, hungry, and being
asked whether he'd rather be useful or die. He chose useful. Rami keeps him close and tells him very little.

He still throws the parties. Now they're how he eats. He has rules about who: the frat boys, the crypto kids, the
guys who wander into the scene like it owes them something. He runs packages across the city for the Pony Express.
He runs with [[31-trieste|Trieste]] and her crew, mostly. He has hit the Sheriff with a car, and shot him in the head, and
walked into his candelabrum, and is still here.

He doesn't think much about the arrangement. That's the idea.
