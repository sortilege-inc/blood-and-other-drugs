Where Ethan grew up there were two careers on offer: the law, or the thing the law was for. He picked
the one that paid on Fridays. Bail bonds. A man bailed him out once, cash on the table at two in the
morning, and kept him around after, and taught him the trade. Bail doesn't keep office hours; nobody at
work noticed when he stopped needing the days.

[[22-rose|Rose]] made him. The blood came out thin, and they both know it, and she is teaching him anyway:
the Traditions, the players, who to tip your hat to crossing the Prince's ground and who not to cross at
all. He is her muscle. He puts himself between her and whatever is happening, and finds out what it is
afterward. He has been set on fire once for it, and it fucking hurt.

He runs the office like a place that expects to be broken into: bars behind the glass door, steel shutters,
a holding cell, cameras on everything. It is moving now, to a building on the coterie's own ground, with a
basement that has no windows at all.

He is not, by his own reckoning, the brightest crayon in the box. He watches people instead of things, and
he is usually right about them.
